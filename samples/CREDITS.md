# Sample credits

The three guitar folders are REAL recorded instrument notes from the
[nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments](https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments)
sample collection (MIT-licensed code; samples gathered by that project from
free/community sources — VSCO2 Community Edition, University of Iowa MIS and
freesound.org — all free to use and redistribute).

The bass, keys and pad folders are per-note MP3 renders obtained from the
[gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts)
project (MIT): basses from the **MusyngKite** soundfont, keys and pad from
the **FluidR3 General MIDI** soundfont by Frank Wen.

| Folder | Instrument | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `guitar/` | Acoustic guitar, steel (recorded) | tonejs-instruments |
| `eguitar/` | Electric guitar, clean (recorded) | tonejs-instruments |
| `nylon/` | Nylon-string guitar (recorded) | tonejs-instruments |
| `bass/` | Electric Bass (finger) | MusyngKite |
| `bassp/` | Electric Bass (pick) | MusyngKite |
| `keys/` | Electric Piano 1 | FluidR3 GM |
| `pad/` | Pad 2 (warm) | FluidR3 GM |
| `piano2/` | Grand piano (recorded — Salamander Grand by Alexander Holm, CC-BY) | tonejs-instruments |
| `epiano/` | Electric Piano 1 | MusyngKite |
| `organ/` | Drawbar Organ | MusyngKite |

FluidR3 (c) Frank Wen; MP3 renders via midi-js-soundfonts (c) Benjamin
Gleitzman. Drum sounds in GuitarLab remain synthesized in Web Audio (no
samples).
