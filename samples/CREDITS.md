# Sample credits

All instrument samples in this folder are per-note MP3 renders obtained from
the [gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts)
project (MIT). The guitar and bass folders are rendered from the
**MusyngKite** soundfont; keys and pad from the **FluidR3 General MIDI**
soundfont by Frank Wen. All MIT-licensed.

| Folder | Instrument | Soundfont |
|---|---|---|
| `guitar/` | Acoustic Guitar (steel) | MusyngKite |
| `eguitar/` | Electric Guitar (clean) | MusyngKite |
| `nylon/` | Acoustic Guitar (nylon) | MusyngKite |
| `bass/` | Electric Bass (finger) | MusyngKite |
| `bassp/` | Electric Bass (pick) | MusyngKite |
| `keys/` | Electric Piano 1 | FluidR3 GM |
| `pad/` | Pad 2 (warm) | FluidR3 GM |

FluidR3 (c) Frank Wen; MP3 renders via midi-js-soundfonts (c) Benjamin
Gleitzman. MIT-licensed: free to use, modify, and redistribute with this
notice. Drum sounds in GuitarLab remain synthesized in Web Audio (no samples).
