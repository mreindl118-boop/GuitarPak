soundLAB 2.0 · the new look, drawn

Four questions before we build

soundLAB 2.0 gets the look you chose: a dark stage, one amber accent, and your instrument as the largest thing on the page. Most of it had never been drawn. So nothing is built yet: everything here is a drawing that you can open and tap.

Four questions need your yes, or a few words on what to change. Each is asked beside its picture, and the part of the app it is about is built only after your answer.

After them come 19 smaller points. Those go ahead as written unless you say otherwise.

Each small picture is the real drawing, made smaller. Tap the line above a picture to open it alone at this device’s own size.

What changed since this was planned

The plan was written before soundLAB 2.1. Since then 2.1 has added the Voice page (the Train tab is now Woodshed, Voice, Tuner), MIDI guitar (found by one strum, in Settings) and the weak-spot map in Exercises. The drawings follow that: the Train tab has its three pages and Settings shows the MIDI guitar line. The Voice page itself and the weak-spot map are not drawn; they get the new look in a step of their own.

One more change is open and not merged yet (pull request #112). It turns the clef button into a small button that shows the key and the tempo on every page. That is a first step toward the key bar drawn here, so question 1 still decides where it ends up.

The key bar

Is this the bar?

What is proposed

Key, tempo, Scale lock, LUMI lights and Help sit in one bar under the header, on every page. The bar stays when the header slides away. On a phone it is five small buttons, and each opens a panel. From tablet width up the Scale lock says in words what it does: “ON · only A Dorian notes sound”. Each time you flip the lock, a short message says it too. The time signature moves into the metronome.

The home

Where does the home sit: as a page of its own that the logo leads to (proposed), or as “Today” inside Learn?

What is proposed

A page of its own. It has one headline (the next thing to practise), one button, your instrument, and one line of numbers. Question 4 of 4 · waits for your answer

The lit notes and their colours

Is this how “play this now”, “next” and the root should look? And which colours: today’s, where the root is amber like the Start button, or the mockup’s, where the root is red?

What is proposed

Four signs, the same on keys, neck and pads. A dot means the note is in the scale. A filled key (on the neck, a dot with a bright ring) means play this now. An empty ring means next. A thicker ring is the root. Right and wrong are a green tick and a red cross, never a colour alone, so all of it can be told apart without colour. The colours stay today’s unless you pick the other set.

  • Keyboard, all sizes
  • Guitar neck
  • Pads
  • The root as the note to play, beside Start
  • The same in grey
  • Every state of a key
  • The five Help levels
  • Every picture of the instruments

How the two sets read beside the Start buttonIn today’s colours the root and the Start button are almost the same amber, so a root that is the note to play looks like a second Start button. In the mockup’s colours the root is a pinkish red and stands apart, but its second and third notes are orange and sit close to the amber too.

Not settledTwo letters on a dot are a little harder to read than we aim for, with dark or with light ink: “♭7” in the mockup’s colours and “A♯” when notes are coloured by name. The drawing puts a thin dark outline around those letters. Say if that is good enough.♭7 in the mockup’s coloursColoured by note name

What these drawings do not show

Four things to keep in mind while you look. None of them needs an answer.

  • Seen in one browser only. Every drawing was looked at in Chrome’s engine on a computer, with a pretended touch screen for the phone and tablet sizes. Nothing was tried on a real phone, iPad or Windows PC, nor in Safari or Firefox. If something looks wrong on your device, that is worth a line in your answer.
  • Still pictures. How a button looks under the mouse, while it is pressed, and when the keyboard’s focus is on it is written down but not drawn.
  • The pads on a LUMI. The pads drawing shows the screen. On a LUMI the eight pad keys light in one orange and flash white on a hit, not in the eight colours of the picture. That is as today and is not changed in this phase.
  • Not drawn yet. The Voice page, the weak-spot map in Exercises, and the home for Voice, Synth and Other.
